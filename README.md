## Hi there 👋 We're Teched Studios

**Innovating Digital Experiences — Web • Mobile • AI**

Teched Studios is an independent technology and creative venture focused on building innovative digital experiences, exploring artificial intelligence, and bringing original ideas to life.

- 🔭 We're currently working on **AI, software, and digital products.**
- 🌱 We're exploring **AI architectures, machine learning, and creative technologies.**
- 👯 We're open to collaborating with **developers, artists, and storytellers.**
- 🧠 Our projects include **DOMINO Architecture, Teched Adaptive Defense, and Yuri.**
- 🎨 Through **Studio Gijutsu**, we're exploring original manga, storytelling, and creative IP.
- 💬 Ask us about **AI research, software development, and creative projects.**
- 📫 Reach us at **techedstudios.contact@gmail.com**
- 🌐 Visit us: **https://techedstudios.in**

⚡ *Building tomorrow, one idea at a time.*
